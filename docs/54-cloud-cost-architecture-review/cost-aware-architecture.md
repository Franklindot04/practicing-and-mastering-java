# Cost-Aware Architecture

Cost-aware architecture means considering cost as part of design, not as an afterthought.

## Right-Sizing Basics

Right-sizing means choosing capacity that matches current needs with room for expected growth. Over-sizing wastes money. Under-sizing can create outages.

## Managed Service Cost Tradeoffs

Managed services can reduce operational burden but may cost more than raw infrastructure. They can also save money by reducing maintenance, backup, patching, and incident time.

## Free Tier And Quota Awareness

Free tiers and quotas are learning aids, not architecture plans. A design should note what happens when traffic, storage, logs, or requests exceed free limits.

## Scaling Cost Risks

Autoscaling can protect availability, but it can also multiply cost during bugs, retry storms, attacks, or batch jobs. Scaling rules need limits, alerts, and review.

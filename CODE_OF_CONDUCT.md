# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making this repository a respectful, inclusive, and welcoming learning space.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and constructive.
- Respect different learning speeds and backgrounds.
- Give feedback that helps people improve.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions.
- Keep discussions focused on learning Java and building useful resources.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Dismissive comments toward beginners.
- Spam or intentionally disruptive behavior.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject pull requests, or restrict participation when behavior harms the learning environment.
